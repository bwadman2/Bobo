Bobo and the Bus

As told by Shannon, from the driver's seat

Shannon: “Everybody sit down and buckle up, because you will not believe what Bobo did this morning.”

“He got loose down at the fish docks, and he was hungry. Bananas everywhere, seagulls everywhere. And the only way home was my bus.”

Bananas 0 / 10 Time 75

Ready, Bobo?

Grab 10 bananas before the bell. Dodge the seagulls, they knock bananas loose. Then run for Shannon's bus!

Phone: touch and hold where you want Bobo to go. Computer: arrow keys or W A S D.

A gift for Shannon. Bobo was a real gorilla raised by an Anacortes family from 1951 to 1953 before moving to Seattle's Woodland Park Zoo. Today's adventure is pure bus-route legend.
